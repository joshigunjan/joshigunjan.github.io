About Me

Machine learning for interpretable Earth observation.

I am Gunjan Joshi, a Machine-Learning Scientist at Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf, Germany. My work focuses on explainable and multimodal AI for Earth observation, especially where different sensors reveal different parts of the same environmental story.

Profile

Current role and background

At HZDR, I work on geospatial foundation models and multimodal learning for Earth observation, including the 3D-ABC Foundation Model for global above- and below-ground carbon-stock mapping. My broader research connects explainable AI, SAR and optical remote sensing, and trustworthy geospatial data systems.

I earned my Ph.D. and M.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Tokyo, where I worked on inverse mapping for neural-network explainability and its applications in remote sensing. This line of work continues in my current research on confidence-aware interpretation, complex-valued models, cryosphere monitoring, and metadata-aware Earth observation datasets.
