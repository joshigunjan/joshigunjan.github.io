About Me

Making Earth-observation AI more interpretable.

I am Gunjan Joshi, a Machine-Learning Scientist at Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf, Germany. My work focuses on explainable and multimodal AI for Earth observation, including SAR/optical data fusion, glacier monitoring, disaster mapping, and geospatial foundation models for carbon-stock estimation.

My path into this field began with electrical engineering, RF systems, and signal processing, and later moved toward remote sensing, neural networks, and explainable AI. That background shapes how I think about Earth observation: not only as images and models, but as sensing systems whose physical meaning should remain understandable.
