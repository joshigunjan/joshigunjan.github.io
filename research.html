Research Themes

Explainable AI

Inverse mapping, confidence-aware interpretation, and trustworthy machine learning for Earth observation.

SAR and optical fusion

Learning from complementary satellite sensors across spectral, structural, polarimetric, and all-weather observations.

Complex and quaternion neural networks

Complex-valued and quaternion neural networks for PolSAR, InSAR, and interpretable remote-sensing models.

Cryosphere monitoring

Glacier surface classification, snowline estimation, and mountain-environment mapping using multi-sensor EO data.

Geospatial foundation models

Multimodal representation learning for Earth observation, carbon-stock modelling, and environmental intelligence.

Metadata and disaster mapping

Metadata quality, reproducibility, disaster assessment, land-cover analysis, and trustworthy EO workflows.
