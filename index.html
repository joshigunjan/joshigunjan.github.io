About

Machine learning for interpretable Earth observation

I am a Machine-Learning Scientist at Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR), Germany. My work focuses on explainable and multimodal AI for Earth observation, especially where different sensors reveal different parts of the same environmental story.

I earned my Ph.D. and M.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Tokyo, where I worked on inverse mapping for neural-network explainability and its applications in remote sensing. My current work extends this direction toward geospatial foundation models, metadata-aware EO datasets, cryosphere monitoring, and global carbon-stock mapping.
