Turning multi-sensor Earth observations into interpretable science.

I am a Machine-Learning Scientist at Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR), Germany, where I work on foundation models for Earth observation. I earned my Ph.D. and M.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Tokyo, specializing in explainable AI for remote sensing.

My research connects interpretable machine learning, SAR and optical remote sensing, and geospatial foundation models. I am especially interested in problems where different sensors reveal different parts of the same environmental story, including glaciers, disaster monitoring, and global carbon-stock mapping.

I also care about how we communicate science. Through IEEE GRSS, editorial work, webinars, teaching, and outreach, I try to make remote sensing and AI more understandable, reproducible, and useful for the wider community.
